TEAM UPDATE PIN — 24 October 2026


WHAT IT IS

  A note from Sober Book that sits at the top of everybody's wall until
  you replace it or take it down. Dark green header strip with a pin,
  the Sober Book name with the official seal under it, and your words.

  You post it from /admin/update — there is a "Team update" button on
  the admin numbers screen next to Artists.


WHAT IT IS NOT, AND WHY

  No account, no profile page, no login. You said the pin was the part
  you wanted, and an account would have brought a password for two
  people to share, a row in the member count, replies and friend
  requests nothing is listening to, and a name people tap through to an
  empty page. All of that is machinery built to hold one sentence.

  The name on the card is deliberately not a link. There is nowhere for
  it to go, and a name that looks tappable and isn't reads as broken.
  If you ever do want the profile page, that is where the link goes.

HOW IT BEHAVES

  · One pin at a time. Posting a new one clears the old one in the same
    database statement, so there is never a moment with two or none.
  · Nothing is deleted. Cleared notes stay in the table with the time
    they came down and which admin posted them.
  · No notification goes out. People see it next time they open the
    wall. An outage is worth waking 449 phones for; a feature is not.
  · 800 characters. It is a notice, not a blog post.
  · Plain text only — never markup. That string renders on 449 people's
    wall, and the day it accepts HTML is the day the wall has an
    injection point on it. Line breaks still survive.
