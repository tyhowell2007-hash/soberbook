WHY IT MATTERS

  sharp 0.33.5 carries three CVEs — two high, one critical — coming out
  of libvips, libheif and librsvg underneath it. Four route handlers
  import it, including app/api/photo/upload/route.js and
  app/api/photo/finalize/route.js. So every photo any of the 449
  members uploads is decoded by those versions.

  It is not theoretical in the way most dependency warnings are: the
  vulnerable code is the thing reading a stranger's image file.
